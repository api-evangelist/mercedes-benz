Mercedes-Benz is a renowned luxury automobile manufacturer that produces a wide range of vehicles, from sedans and SUVs to sports cars and electric vehicles. Founded in 1926 in Germany, the company is known for its commitment to innovation, quality, and performance. Mercedes-Benz constantly pushes the boundaries of automotive technology, offering cutting-edge features such as advanced driver-assistance systems and state-of-the-art infotainment systems.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Mercedes-Benz: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
